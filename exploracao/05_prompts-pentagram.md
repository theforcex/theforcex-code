## O método que estou aplicando

Pentagram quase nunca faz símbolo decorativo. O padrão é: **uma ideia só, dita na tipografia, com rigor e contenção** — e a ideia tem que ser legível no próprio desenho (Mastercard, Windows, Yale Art Gallery, MIT Media Lab, Warner Bros). Nada de dois conceitos empilhados, nada de ornamento.

Então cada prompt abaixo carrega **um único movimento**. Se precisar de duas frases para explicar, está errado.

# CONCEITO A — A luz só se materializa quando observada

*"in NATURE, when not observed, light behaves like a wave… only when it's observed light fully materializes… the light is the present, the sand is the memory."*

A ideia de marca: **a Téra é o ato de observação**. O logo tem que conter a passagem onda → partícula, indefinido → definido.

### A1 · Difração
O núcleo sólido com as franjas de difração caindo simetricamente para os lados. A palavra é nítida; a luz que ela emite é que se decompõe.
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera" with an acute accent over the e, letters solid and sharp
at the core, each letter flanked by symmetrical diffraction fringes: progressively
thinner and more spaced vertical bands falling off to left and right. The word stays
perfectly legible; the fringes are its halo. Flat black on white, one single color,
no gradients, no shadows, no blur, no texture.
```

### A2 · A fenda dupla
O acento agudo vira **duas fendas verticais**. Nada mais no logo muda — o experimento inteiro num diacrítico.
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera", rigorously solid geometric letterforms. Over the e, in
place of a normal acute accent, two identical narrow vertical slits side by side.
Everything else in the word is plain and quiet. Flat black on white, one single
color, no gradients, no shadows, no texture.
```

### A3 · Onda → partícula
Uma transição contínua ao longo das quatro letras: o `t` ainda é onda (listrado), o `a` já é matéria (sólido).
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera" with an acute accent, rendered as a continuous transition
across the four letters: the first letter is built from thin horizontal stripes with
white gaps, and each following letter becomes progressively more solid until the last
letter is completely filled. One single left-to-right progression. Flat black on
white, one color, no gradients, no shadows.
```

### A4 · O grão
*"when observed light becomes a single particle."* O acento é um ponto perfeito. Só isso.
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera", calm geometric letterforms of even weight. The acute accent
over the e is replaced by one single perfect circular dot, small and precisely placed.
The dot is the only incident in the whole mark. Flat black on white, one single color,
no gradients, no shadows, no texture.
```

### A5 · Interferência na contraforma
As letras são sólidas; dentro dos olhos delas correm os anéis de interferência.
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera" with an acute accent, letters solid black with generous
closed counters. Inside each closed counter, concentric interference rings drawn as
thin lines. Outside the letters, pure white and nothing else. Flat black on white,
one single color, no gradients, no shadows.
```

# CONCEITO B — Dobrar o Tecido da Realidade

*Escala · Solo · Extraordinário → Espetáculos Multidimensionais → Tempo · Perspectiva · Participação → Realidade Expandida → Continuidade · Relação → Tecido da Realidade → **Dobra**.*

A ideia de marca: **a dobra reconfigura sem romper a continuidade.** O logo tem que dobrar sem quebrar.

### B1 · Uma dobra, a palavra inteira
Um vinco diagonal atravessa o wordmark; acima e abaixo dele a palavra desloca. Continuidade preservada, configuração alterada.
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera" with an acute accent, bold geometric letterforms, cut by
one single straight diagonal crease running across the entire word. Above the crease
the letters sit upright; below it the whole lower half is shifted sideways by a small
constant offset. The letters remain continuous and readable across the crease. Flat
black on white, one single color, no gradients, no shadows.
```

### B2 · O acento dobrado
O acento não é um traço: é um plano dobrado. Um vinco, dois valores do mesmo preto.
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera", quiet even geometric letterforms. The acute accent over
the e is a small folded plane: a parallelogram creased down the middle, one half solid
black and the other half outlined, so it reads as a sheet of paper folded. Everything
else in the mark is plain. Flat black on white, no gradients, no shadows, no texture.
```

### B3 · O tecido puxado
A grade que constrói as letras se deforma ao atravessá-las — o campo contínuo sendo esticado.
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera" with an acute accent, letterforms built from a fine regular
grid of thin lines. Where the grid crosses the letters it bends and stretches smoothly,
as if the fabric is being pulled from one point; outside the letters the grid stays
perfectly regular. Flat black on white, one single color, no gradients, no shadows.
```

### B4 · O t de uma dobra só
Monograma: um `t` feito de uma única fita contínua dobrada. Sem começo nem emenda.
```
Minimal logo monogram: a single lowercase letter t formed by one continuous strip of
material folded on itself, with visible straight fold lines where it changes direction.
No breaks, no separate pieces, the strip reads as one uninterrupted ribbon. Flat black
on white, one single color, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D rendering.
```

### B5 · Continuidade
As quatro letras desenhadas por uma linha só, que dobra sobre si mesma e nunca se interrompe.
```
Lowercase wordmark "tera" with an acute accent, drawn as one single unbroken line of
constant thickness that folds back on itself to form all four letters in sequence.
The line never lifts and never crosses itself. Sharp angular folds, no curves where a
fold can do the work. Flat black on white, one color, no gradients, no shadows.
```
